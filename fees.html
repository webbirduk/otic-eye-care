Pricing & Policy FAQ

Is there a consultation fee?

If the clinician examines your ears and determines that no treatment (microsuction) is necessary or safe at that time, a reduced consultation fee of £100 applies instead of the full treatment price.

Will I be charged if you can't remove all the wax?

Our ENT doctors are highly skilled and successfully remove wax in 99% of cases. If the wax is exceptionally hard and requires further softening with oil, we will return for a follow-up visit at a significantly reduced rate.

What is your cancellation policy?

We require at least 24 hours' notice for cancellations. Appointments cancelled with less than 24 hours' notice may be subject to a cancellation fee to cover the clinician's travel and scheduling costs.
