Booking FAQ

How quickly can I get an appointment?

We pride ourselves on our responsiveness. Most standard appointments are fulfilled within 24 hours, and emergency visits can often be arranged for the same evening.

Can I cancel or reschedule my booking?

Yes, you can reschedule or cancel your appointment by contacting us at least 24 hours in advance. Please refer to our full terms at the time of booking for our cancellation policy.

Is there a travel fee?

Our standard fees include travel within all central London boroughs. For locations in the Home Counties (outside the M25), a small travel surcharge may apply, which will be confirmed during the booking process.
