What is microsuction?

‘Micro’ – because we use microscopes, ‘suction’ – because we use a finely calibrated suction device, to vacuum out the wax.

Our microsuction equipment is the best in class. We use Carl Zeiss optics and suction units designed and manufactured by DeVilBiss Healthcare, who are world leaders in the production of medical devices for ear cleaning.

The procedure will normally take a maximum of 30 minutes. The most you should feel is a gentle pulling sensation as your ear is being cleaned.

Ear Wax Removal FAQ

Does microsuction hurt?

No, microsuction is generally painless. You will hear a sucking sound (like a small vacuum) and may feel a gentle pulling sensation, but it is much safer and more comfortable than traditional syringing.

Do I need to use oil before the appointment?

Using olive oil (Earol) for 2–3 days prior to your appointment can help soften very hard wax, making removal even faster and more comfortable, though it is not always mandatory.

How long does the appointment take?

The full consultation and treatment usually take between 20 to 30 minutes, depending on the volume and hardness of the wax.
