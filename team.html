Clinical Team FAQ

Are your doctors GMC registered?

Yes, all clinicians at Otic Ear Care are fully registered with the General Medical Council (GMC) and hold active licenses to practice in the UK. We handle all medical cases with the highest level of professional accountability.

What does CQC regulation mean?

The Care Quality Commission (CQC) is the independent regulator of all health and social care services in England. Being CQC-regulated means our clinic is strictly monitored to ensure we provide safe, effective, and high-quality care.

How often is your advisory board involved?

Our advisory board meets regularly to review clinical audits, safety protocols, and prescribing policies. This ensures our practice remains at the cutting edge of Otolaryngology standards.
