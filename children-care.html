Why Children May Need Ear Wax Removal

While routine wax often clears itself, some children experience symptoms of build-up that can interfere with speech developement and concentration at school.

  • Reduced hearing or difficulty hearing
  • Tugging or scratching at the ears
  • Irritability during play or bedtime
  • Repeated ear infections

Children's Ear Care FAQ

Is microsuction safe for my child?

Yes, microsuction is the gold-standard method for ear cleaning in children. It is dry, highly precise, and allows the doctor to see the entire ear canal under high magnification, ensuring absolute safety.

What if my child won't stay still?

Our specialists are experts in pediatric care. We use distraction techniques and take our time to allow your child to become comfortable with the equipment. Most children tolerate the procedure remarkably well when in their own home.

Are parents allowed to stay during the visit?

Absolutely. We encourage parents to be present. Your child can sit on your lap or next to you during the treatment to help them feel secure.
