Is microsuction painful?
No. Microsuction is widely considered the safest, most comfortable, and pain-free method of ear wax removal. You may feel a gentle pull or hear a slight whooshing sound from the suction machine, but it should not cause any pain.
Do I need to put in olive oil drops before you visit?
While it can be helpful if wax is heavily impacted, it is not strictly necessary for our ENT doctors to use microsuction successfully. We carry clinical-grade drops and instruments to soften wax safely on-site if required.
Are there any hidden travel fees?
No hidden fees. When you enter your postcode during the booking process, your final fee includes all travel expenses for the ENT doctor directly to your home anywhere across our London service network.
How long does an appointment take?
Usually, an appointment takes around 20–30 minutes, though we allocate ample time to ensure you are never rushed. We ensure comprehensive cleaning and provide post-procedure advice before departing.
