What ear care services do you provide?
We specialize in mobile microsuction ear wax removal, ear infection management, safe foreign body extraction, and dedicated pediatric ear cleaning for children and babies across London.
Are you available 24/7?
Yes. We provide standard clinical visits throughout the day and operate a specialized emergency mobile clinic service between 7:00pm and 7:00am daily.
Which areas of London do you cover?
We cover the entire Greater London area, including North, East, West, North West, South East, and South West London, as well as the Home Counties.
Do I need a GP referral?
No referral is necessary. You can book an appointment directly with our ENT specialists via our website or by calling our clinical team.
Is your service strictly mobile?
Yes, we are a dedicated mobile service. We bring hospital-grade microsuction equipment and high-resolution microscopy directly to your home or office for maximum convenience.
How does microsuction ear wax removal work?
Microsuction uses a gentle suction device and high-resolution microscopy to safely remove wax without water. It is the cleanest and safest method currently available.
How long does a typical appointment take?
A comprehensive clinical session typically takes between 20 to 30 minutes, ensuring all wax is removed and your ears are thoroughly examined.
Do I need to use ear drops before my appointment?
While 2-3 days of olive oil drops can help soften the wax, our clinicians carry specialized softening tools and can often complete the procedure without prior preparation.
Will ear microsuction be painful?
No, microsuction is completely painless. You may hear a suction sound or feel a slight tickle, but most patients find it very comfortable compared to old-fashioned syringing.
Can you treat ear infections during the visit?
Yes. Our ENT doctors can diagnose infections on-site and provide targeted intervention, including cleaning the infected area and advising on appropriate medication.
Are you regulated by the CQC?
Yes, Auris Ear Care is fully regulated by the Care Quality Commission (CQC), ensuring we meet the highest national standards for safety and quality of care.
Are your clinicians qualified ENT doctors?
Yes, all our clinicians are highly experienced Ear, Nose, and Throat (ENT) specialists with medical degrees and extensive clinical backgrounds.
Is microsuction safe for children and babies?
Absolutely. We have a dedicated pediatric service and our clinicians are experts in gentle microsuction for babies, toddlers, and older children.
What are your sterilization protocols?
We use single-use, sterile clinical instruments for every patient. Our specialists follow strict hospital-grade hygiene protocols to ensure absolute patient safety.
What if I have a perforated eardrum?
Microsuction is the only safe method for wax removal if you have a perforated eardrum, as it does not involve the use of water or pressure.
How much does ear wax removal cost?
Our pricing is transparent and includes the home visit. Please check our booking page for the current flat-rate fee for standard clinical sessions.
Is there an additional fee for emergencies?
Yes, emergency mobile appointments between 7:00pm and 7:00am incur an additional clinical fee of £175 on top of the standard treatment cost.
Are there travel charges for my area?
Travel within our core London zones is included. Some areas may incur a £50-£100 travel fee, while locations outside London have a minimum £175 travel charge.
How do I book an appointment?
You can book instantly via our secure online system by clicking 'Book Now', or call our clinical team directly for emergency or same-day visits.
How quickly can you visit?
We often provide same-day appointments. For emergencies, our mobile team aims to reach you as quickly as possible, usually within a few hours.
