Microsuction Ear Wax Removal

What is microsuction?

‘Micro’ – because we use microscopes, ‘suction’ – because we use a finely calibrated suction device, to vacuum out the wax.

Our microsuction equipment is the best in class. We use Carl Zeiss optics and suction units designed and manufactured by DeVilBiss Healthcare, who are world leaders in the production of medical devices for ear cleaning.

Occasionally, when microsuction alone is not possible, we use a combination of fine instruments and eardrops; for example, in cases of impacted ear wax or foreign body removal.

The procedure will normally take a maximum of 30 minutes. The most you should feel is a gentle pulling sensation as your ear is being cleaned.

Why microsuction?

We only practice microsuction, which is the NHS gold-standard for ear wax removal and is supported by NICE guidance.

We are the UK’s first CQC regulated provider of microsuction for mobile ear wax removal in London.

Unlike ear candling, syringing or irrigation, microsuction has a significantly lower risk of causing damage to your eardrum.

It is also an important part of the treatment of swimmer’s ear, or otitis externa, which can cause discharge and itchy ears.

Safer, cleaner and better tolerated by our patients.

Once the procedure is complete, our patients notice an immediate relief of their symptoms and often an instant improvement in hearing, from the comfort of their home. No more blocked ears!

Is microsuction painful?
No. Microsuction is widely considered the safest, most comfortable, and pain-free method of ear wax removal. You may feel a gentle pull or hear a slight whooshing sound from the suction machine, but it should not cause any pain.
Do I need to put in olive oil drops before you visit?
While it can be helpful if wax is heavily impacted, it is not strictly necessary for our ENT doctors to use microsuction successfully. We carry clinical-grade drops and instruments to soften wax safely on-site if required.
Are there any hidden travel fees?
No hidden fees. When you enter your postcode during the booking process, your final fee includes all travel expenses for the ENT doctor directly to your home anywhere across our London service network.
How long does an appointment take?
Usually, an appointment takes around 20–30 minutes, though we allocate ample time to ensure you are never rushed. We ensure comprehensive cleaning and provide post-procedure advice before departing.
