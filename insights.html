Common Questions

How often should I have my ears cleaned?

For most people, ear cleaning is only necessary when wax causes symptoms like hearing loss or discomfort. However, hearing aid users or those with narrow ear canals may benefit from a check-up every 6–12 months.

Can I safely clean my ears at home?

We strongly advise against using cotton buds or home irrigation kits, as these can push wax deeper or damage the eardrum. The safest way to manage wax is through professional microsuction.

Do you offer medical certificates?

Yes, our doctors can provide clinical assessment summaries or 'fit to fly' advice following your treatment if required for travel or occupational health.
